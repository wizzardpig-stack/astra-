Western tropical zodiac: signs are measured from the spring equinox.

Positions come from standard astronomical series — Meeus solar and lunar theory, and JPL Keplerian elements for Mercury through Pluto — computed for your exact moment in UTC and precessed to the date.     WHERE THESE NUMBERS COME FROM

    Every placement in your chart contributes to three functions: Generator initiates, Operator sustains, Destroyer ends. The modalities map onto them directly — cardinal initiates, fixed sustains, mutable dissolves — and each element is distributed across all three rather than assigned to one.

    Placements are weighted by how personal they are. Sun, Moon and Ascendant count most; Uranus, Neptune and Pluto count least, because almost everyone born in the same few years shares them. The three figures are shares of one whole and add to 100%. Even thirds would be 33.3% each — the marker on each bar.

    This is an original framework, not traditional astrology. It is a way of reading the chart, offered as one.
